ABOUT THE PROJECT

A Bridge Between Citizens and Local Services.

Wolaita Reporter gives residents of Sodo and the wider Wolaita zone a simple way to report local problems, and a transparent way to follow what happens next.

WHY WE BUILT THIS

Reporting a Problem Shouldn't Be Hard

Today, someone who notices a broken streetlight, a damaged road, or a blocked drainage system often has no clear way to report it, and even when they do, they rarely find out what happened afterward. Wolaita Reporter is built to fix that, by creating one clear place to report a problem and track its progress from submission to resolution.

THE REPORT LIFECYCLE

From Report to Resolution

01

Submitted

You report a problem with a category, title, description, and location.

02

Under Review

The report is reviewed before action is taken.

03

In Progress

Work is underway to address the reported problem.

04

Resolved

You can check back anytime and see when the problem is fixed.
